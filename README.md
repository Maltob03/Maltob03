### What I do

- 🎓 **Teach.** I'm a Junior Mentor in Coding at the Apple Developer Academy in Naples and an Apple Certified Trainer. I mentor international teams from their first prototype to an App Store release, and I give workshops in English on Swift, app architecture, accessibility and AI/ML.
- ✍️ **Write.** I'm a contributing editor at [Create with Swift](https://www.createwithswift.com/author/matteo/), with 85+ articles and tutorials and 90+ issues of the [weekly newsletter](https://www.createwithswift.com/subscribe/), where I pick the *Indie App of the Week*.
- 🛠️ **Build.** I've shipped apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac, from an AR anatomy lab to a calculator for blind users.
